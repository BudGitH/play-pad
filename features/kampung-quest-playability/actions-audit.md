# Kampung Quest: actions audit and "one way to do each thing" spec

> Read-only audit of `games/kampung-quest/` at `1607770` (no game code touched). Evidence screenshots: `shots/actions-before-{desk,phone}-NN-*.png` (script: `shoot-actions.mjs`). Owner: "Users are confused about the actions ... rest, the hovering part, the praying part ... 7-year-olds are frustrated." Feeds AC-P24 / AC-P25 in `requirements.md`.

**Hypothesis verdict: HELD, with two corrections.** "Hovering" is the floating Talk/action button (`kq-talk`): it is re-projected every frame over the head of whatever target wins, and its word, icon, colour and even its screen position change with the target (`interact.js:276-303`, `loop.js:97-99`, `hud-layout.js:104-124`). Corrections: (1) "Rest" is not only the dialog: the Rest button hovers on the hero at the home door even at full energy and **during the azan** (shot `phone-08`), and the Tired bar has no action. (2) Praying is worse than "many entry points": there are 4 different verbs for one job (Wudu / Pray / Sunat prayer / Show me the way) and wudu and prayer are two separate trips.

## 2. Confusion points, ranked (7-year-old, pre-/early reader)

1. **The hover button changes identity.** One element, one fixed corner of nothing: it floats on the target's head, so it moves with the camera, jumps when the winner changes, and is nudged away from toasts (`fitTalk`). Its icon/word/colour swap between Talk, Order, Wudu, Pray, Sunat prayer, Rest, Drive (`ICON_FOR` `interact.js:244-264`). A child cannot learn "the big button is where I press". Evidence: `desk-02-home` (Rest on the hero's head), `desk-05-hall`, `phone-01-counter`.
2. **Pray has 4 verbs and 5 doors.** Wudu (taps) -> walk -> Pray (hall) is two separate prompts; the status card opens a 4-button dialog of long sentences ("Wudu and pray right here"); the azan card says "Show me the way"; the hall says "Sunat prayer" (an unreadable, unexplained word) when nothing is due. Stepping into the hall without wudu gives only a toast (`prayer-flow.js:752-756,720-736`). Evidence: `phone-08-azan`, `phone-09-daycard`, `desk-05-hall`.
3. **Rest is a trip, a dialog and a surprise.** The prompt shows at full energy (nothing to rest for); it competes with the azan on the same hero (`phone-08`); the Tired bar is a dead end (the only help is a toast, `nudge.js:22-24`, and unlike food there is no beacon, `nudge.js:87-92`); the dialog has three ways out (Rest, Not now, X); time jumps 30 min and the sun may change, which is unexplained (`desk-03`). Needs only slow the hero to 85 % (`needs.js:142,207`), so the stakes are invisible.
4. **"Walk closer" is a button that does nothing.** A dashed disabled button is a lie to a child who taps it (`interact.js:145-149`); it is also hidden off-screen, so the NPC just seems unresponsive.
5. **Silent priority between targets.** Hawker NPC stands ~0.15 m behind the counter; nenek-with-a-mission vs a counter vs a vehicle: the child sees only the winner and cannot choose the other (`interact.js:160-176`). Will get worse with Say hi (people + crowd kids), Ride/Say hi (camels, horses), Drive/Ride/Exit, event actions.
6. **Text is the only channel for hints.** Toasts ("Follow the green footprints...", "Let's take wudu at the taps first...") are text, 3-6 s, held back behind sheets (`ui-core.js:155-194`); read-to-me exists only on cards and is not auto (`speech.js:66`); NPC voices speak only NPC lines (`voice.js:1-17`).
7. **Gold = due, gold = next, pink = action.** The Zohor chip glows gold at 07:00 and the pink Pray chip is always on (`desk-02`), so "Pray" looks urgent when nothing is due; the azan card, status card and hover button are three pink/green things that all mean "go pray".
8. **Prayer is ~57 taps and 2 flows** (28 wudu + 29 posture for Zohor) with an entry that differs by where you stand; a failed attempt gives a toast that points to a different place. (Stepper length itself is out of scope here; the entry must be one tap.)

## 3. Proposed design: one way to do each thing

**Principles:** one fixed Action button; the world only _points_; one picture + one word; never a choice the child did not ask for, but when there are two, show both as pictures; the same picture everywhere (HUD, prompt, sheet title, toast, spoken).

**3.1 Fixed Action control (replaces the hovering Talk button).** A 72 px round button + label in a fixed spot: right edge, directly above Jump on touch (Jump stays, joystick untouched); bottom-right above the key hints on desktop. Contents: big picture, one word, `E` key chip on desktop. States: _idle_ (nothing in reach: faded footprint picture, not tappable), _ready_, _busy_ (stepper open: hidden). Hover in the world becomes a **non-interactive pointer**: a bobbing arrow/ring (`pointer-events:none`, same icon as the button, never a label) above the highlighted target, also shown for NPCs 2.3-4.5 m away (replaces "Walk closer": the arrow _is_ the "go there"). No dashed disabled button anywhere.

**3.2 Chooser tray when 2+ actions are in reach.** The Action control grows upward into a stack of up to 3 picture tiles (>= 56 px, picture + one word), nearest/most-important on the bottom, highlighted; `E`/Enter does the highlighted one, `1-3` or tap does another, Tab/arrows move; the arrow marker follows the highlight. Order = mission-talk > owed prayer > service > Say hi > vehicle/mount > pick-up, then distance; duplicates of the same kind collapse (many crowd kids -> one "Say hi"). It replaces `pickTarget`/`talked`/`closer` as the _chooser_; `Sim.Missions.pickTarget` stays as the pure default order (keep its unit test).

**3.3 Word list (picture-first; one word each; spoken the same).** Talk, Say hi, Eat (counters; was Order), Buy, Pray, Rest, Ride, Drive, Get off (was Exit), Pick up, Play, Sit, Look (library/clinic/tailor keep their current pictures, word Story / Check-up / Clothes). No "Sunat", "Wudu" or "Walk closer" on any button. New actions (Say hi, camel/horse Ride, kick ball, buy ice cream, catch balloon) just need a row in one table `ACTION_WORDS = { label: { icon, word, say } }`; unknown labels fall back to icon `hand` + their own word. They keep emitting today's target shape `{kind,id,label,aria,x,z,dist,enabled,h}` (as `vehicles.js:403-413` does), so the merged features plug into the list without new UI. "Go here" (map) is not an Action tile: it draws the same bobbing arrow marker and the radar blip.

**3.4 Rest = one tap, no dialog.** Tile "Rest" (moon/bed picture) is offered (a) within reach of the home door or any bench, always; (b) **anywhere when Tired** (energy < 25), at the top of the tray; never when a fard prayer is due/being prayed (Pray wins). One tap: hero sits/curls ("Sit & rest" at the spot, no walking), a short ZZZ animation + fade, `Loop.advanceMinutes(30, true)` as today, then a spoken "Energy is back!". No yes/no card; undo is unnecessary (no cost, no fail). The Tired bar and its word become a tappable Rest shortcut (>= 48 px) and the energy-low nudge adds the same pointer to the nearest bench/home instead of text only. At energy >= 95 the tile reads "Sit" (3 s, cosmetic) so Rest is never offered as a no-op.

**3.5 Pray = one path, always "the next right thing".** One function `PrayerFlow.next()` behind **every** door (status card tap, azan card button, hover tile at taps and hall, key P, dock). Rules, using existing flow functions only (`startFlow`, `openWudu`, `openPray`, `showWay`, `startHere`; **no change to `KQ.Sim.Prayer` order/content**):

1. A flow is running -> reopen the right stepper (wudu at the taps/here, prayer in the hall/here).
2. Hero in the mosque compound (within ~25 m of taps/hall; no walking needed) -> `startHere()`: wudu is step 1, then the prayer opens in the same place automatically (`finishWudu` already does when `here`). The child never sees a separate "Wudu" prompt.
3. Hero far away -> one tap draws the green footprints (existing `showWay`) with a spoken "Let's walk to the mosque", and the tray offers one extra tile "Pray here" while the trail is on (rule 2). No dialog.
4. Hall entered without wudu -> do rule 2 (wudu first, automatically) instead of a toast.
   Defaults: a fard/qada prayer owed always outranks the optional sunat (keep `startable`, `dropIdleSunat`, `letSunatGo`). When nothing is owed the button still says **Pray** (picture only; the stepper kicker already names "Sunat prayer (2 raka'at)"); a tap on the status card with nothing owed opens a read-only day card with one button "Pray a little extra". Azan card: button becomes the same Pray tile; day card loses "Show me the way" / long sentences. Gold glow only on the _due_ prayer, not on "next".

**3.6 Spoken hints for non-readers.** New `G.Say(key, text)` = toast + `Speech.speak` (shorter sentences, auto when `prefs.readToMe`, default ON for the first 3 sessions or when the child has not tapped anything for 20 s), obeying the Voice gates (mute, pause, hidden, azan, prayer screen). Used for: first sight of each action ("Tap the big button to talk"), Rest, Pray steps, trail start. Each hint shown once per action kind (`Settings.prefs.seen` booleans, sanitised; not in the save).

**Keep:** key E, touch >= 48 px (button 72, tiles 56), `textContent` only (`U.el`), no fail state, azan silence rules, jump/horn, existing lint (<= 40 lines/function).

## 4. Acceptance criteria to append (refine AC-P24 / AC-P25)

- **AC-P24.1** The Action control has a fixed screen position (same rect +- 2 px) whatever the target, on desktop and phone landscape; no element named Talk/Walk closer floats over the world; the world pointer has `pointer-events:none` and no text.
- **AC-P24.2** Out-of-reach NPCs never produce a clickable "Walk closer"; the pointer arrow shows within 4.5 m.
- **AC-P24.3** With 2+ distinct actions in reach the tray shows up to 3 tiles (picture + one word), the highlight is the default order, `E` runs it, tile tap / `1-3` run another; every tile >= 56 px.
- **AC-P24.4** Every action label comes from one table with icon + word + spoken line; unknown labels degrade to the hand icon + their own word; new actions (Say hi, Ride, Go here marker, events) appear in the tray without a code change to the HUD.
- **AC-P24.5** First time each action kind appears the hint is spoken (when speech is available and allowed by Voice gates) and never twice; never during azan / prayer screen / mute.
- **AC-P25.1** Rest takes exactly one tap from the Action control or the Tired bar, shows no yes/no card, raises energy to >= 95 and advances the clock 30 min; it is offered anywhere when Tired and at home door / benches otherwise, never while a fard prayer is due or in flow.
- **AC-P25.2** From any spot, with the azan due: one tap on the status card, azan card or Action tile makes exactly one thing happen (stepper in the compound, trail when far); wudu then prayer run in one flow without a second prompt; sim state (order, raka'at) identical to today.
- **AC-P25.3** No button, toast or card shows the words "Sunat", "Wudu" as an action label, or "Show me the way" as a second prayer path; the sunat prayer remains available (extra prayer) and never preempts a due fard prayer.
- **AC-P25.4** Existing prayer specs (`kampung-quest-prayer`, `-ac` AC-7..9), mobile HUD no-overlap (10 viewports) and `test:kq` stay green.
