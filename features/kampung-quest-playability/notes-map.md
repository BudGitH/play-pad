## How each AC is met

- **AC-P1 (radar + registry).** `map-markers.js` turns every `radar` kind of the hero's city into a blip (icon disc, kind colour, priority). `minimap.js` keeps the most important icon when two overlap (stalls, bins and car parks are `radar:false`, full map only). Mosque, food, Kaaba and the Prophet's Mosque still ride the rim when out of range. Existing marker kinds (`objective`, `mosque`, `food`, `home`, `giver`) and the `big` / `arrow` / orange `food` contract are unchanged (the HUD / AC specs read them).
- **AC-P2 (full map).** Tap the radar or press M: `G.BigMap.open`. Whole region on a canvas: ground, parks, plazas, streets, footpaths (walk graph), building footprints and trees (colliders), camel yard + caravan track + oasis pond, Haram sites. Hero arrow with heading and a halo, "You" label, the objective as a pink pin (or a pink edge pointer when off screen), a legend that is the Places list, labels on every kept icon.
- **AC-P3 (zoom / pan).** Pinch, wheel, + / - buttons (52 px), drag, "centre on me" (52 px), Places toggle, Close (48 px), Escape, M, backdrop tap, arrows / WASD pan, + / - zoom, `0` shows everything, `C` centres. Clamped to the world (`MapView.fit`); zoom 1x .. 8x of "whole region fits". Focus trap and focus return come from `UI.openSheet`. Input is blocked while it is open the same way as every sheet (`UI.modalOpen`).
- **AC-P4 (go here).** Tap a place: name card, "About 40 m away", **Go here** (or **Stop going here**). The target is a blue radar rim arrow (`goto` marker, `force`, `big`, `arrow`), a blue ground beacon (`markers.js` 4th beacon), a dashed blue route on the full map along the walk graph, and a toast ("Going to ..."). It clears on arrival (4 m; `reach` of 8-38 m for big places such as the Kaaba, the camel yard, the car park), when the hero changes city, or via **Stop going here** / tapping the selected target again. The mission objective stays pink and separate: both show at once.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Tap selects, the button commits.** A tap on a place selects it and shows its card; **Go here** on the card sets the target. (AC-P4 in `requirements.md` reads "tapping a place ... sets it as a go-here target". The brief for this workstream asked for card + button, which also stops a child setting a target by brushing the map while panning.) Tapping the selected target again clears it.
- **Both Haramain cities on one screen type.** A Makkah / Madinah switch in the header; the map opens on the hero's city. A place in the other city shows a card that says to take the bus and offers no Go here (a go-here arrow across the desert would point nowhere useful). Needed `world.poisOf(city)` (one line in `holy-world.js`) because `world.pois` holds only the current city.
- **The Places list is the legend.** Instead of a paragraph of colour words, each row is a 48 px button with the icon chip and the name; it zooms to the place. It is also the keyboard / screen-reader path to every place (the canvas itself is `role=img`). Open by default on wide screens; on phones it floats over the map behind a "Places" button.
- **Level of detail.** Priority >= 7 kinds (stalls, bins, car parks) show only above 3 px / m (the kampung's whole-map view is ~5 px / m so they show there; Makkah's whole-region view is ~2 px / m so the "Cars and bikes" icons wait until you zoom in).
- **Base map paint-once.** Offscreen 1536 px canvas per city (about 9 MB each, made on first open); the overlay (icons, hero, route) is the only thing redrawn, on demand (taps, buttons, keys), at the next frame for drags / wheel, and from the loop's 0.12 s tick only if the hero / target / objective changed. The radar's cost is unchanged apart from <= ~25 icon discs and one small sort.
- `Screens.map`, `Minimap.fullSpan` and `Minimap.fullOpts` stay (the first now delegates; the other two are unused) so nothing external breaks.

## Known gaps / ideas

- The map is not "paused behind" in the sense of the 3D scene: the world keeps rendering under the opaque sheet (like every other sheet). On a low-end iPad a later pass could skip the 3D render while the full map is open.
- Mount / animal icons show only when the animals workstream adds their POIs; until then the camel yard, caravan and oasis come from `HolyCamels` data.
- Landmarks inside the Haram (Maqam Ibrahim, Hijr Ismail, Black Stone) and the sa'i lane's two ends are not separate places; Safa and Marwa are.
- A route line exists only for places that sit on the walk graph (POIs); camel yard / oasis / hotel-in-Madinah get a straight dashed line from the hero.
