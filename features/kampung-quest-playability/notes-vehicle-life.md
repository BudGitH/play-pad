# Notes: vehicle life (get in / out, lights, exhaust, traffic, bus people, sound)

Build notes from the "vehicle life" workstream. Fold into `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the game-local `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/LEARNINGS.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md` at CAPTURE (this workstream did not touch the shared brain docs).

Owner ask (abridged): other characters drive the cars and ride in them; people are seen in the bus, getting on and off; the hero opens the car door and gets in (a GTA-like feel, only the get-in / get-out animation); lights come on when vehicles stop and go; exhaust from petrol and diesel; subtle engine, scooter, bicycle and horn sounds. Audience 6-10: nobody is ever pulled out, nothing crashes, vehicles only slow down.

## Rig API relied on (all guarded: a no-op when absent, so the code works before the looks branch merges)

`js/game/vehicle-rig.js` is the one place that touches it.

- `rig.doors[{side:"L"|"R", seat, pivot, open(t 0..1)}]`: `doorFor(rig, side, seat)` picks the door of the seat's side (preferring the one naming the seat), `open(t)` is called every frame of a scene and with 0 at the end. The bus uses `Rig.setBusDoors` (the new `open(t)` on every door, else the old `F.Vehicles.setDoors` slide).
- `rig.seats[{pos (local Vector3), heading, kind:"driver"|"front"|"rear"|"bus"}]`: used as the seat spots of occupants; `pos.y` > 0.2 is taken as the seat-top height, else the model's. Fallback (today): driver at `seatX`, front passenger, one or two rear rows (`VehicleCars.STYLES[style].rows`), lorry cab seats, 11 bus seats plus a driver's seat.
- `rig.lights.setBrake(on)`, `setHead(on)`, `setReverse(on)`, `setIndicator("L"|"R"|null, t)`: called only when a state changes, except `setIndicator`, which is called every frame while blinking with the game time `t` in seconds (the blink itself is also decided here, `Sim.VehicleLife.blinkOn`; if the looks side expects a phase 0..1 instead, change one line in `vehicle-lights.js indicate`).
- `rig.exhaust {pos, fuel}`: `pos` local, `fuel` "petrol" | "diesel" | "electric". Fallback: pipe at the rear, diesel for the taxi style, the lorry and the bus, petrol otherwise.
- Bicycle `rig.pedals(phase)` (radians of crank), `rig.wheels(spin)`, `rig.steer(angle)`; scooter `wheels` / `steer`: `Rig.animateBike`, called each frame while riding, a no-op unless they are functions.

## Decisions

- **`?enter=0` skips the get-in / get-out scene** (a test and quick-play flag, read once by `VehicleEnter.enabled()`). The three existing vehicle specs and `vehicles-measure.mjs` add it to their URLs: they mount a car and hold the stick a few frames later, which a 1.4 s locked scene would break. The new spec runs with the scene on and uses `Vehicles.finishScene()` when it only needs the seat.
- **Traffic drives the N-S road only (kampung).** The E-W road belongs to the bus: its lanes sit at z = +-0.3 (to clear the parked kerbside cars at |z| 2.6 and the lorry), so a car in the other lane (z = -+0.95) would overlap the bus's 2.4 m body, and a head-on yield between two cars and the bus deadlocks. On the N-S road cars cross the E-W junction and yield to the bus there (`vehicleCap` samples the whole bus and a point one second ahead of it).
- **Makkah: only the two outer streets** (z 85 and z -175), right-hand traffic, driver on the left, each lane checked against `KQ.Holy.inNoDrive` with 6 m clearance (`TrafficRoutes.routes()` drops any street that fails). Madinah's streets run within 5 m of the courtyard, so there is no traffic in Madinah.
- **People in vehicles are crowd instances, not rigs**, drawn from `crowd.js` after the members (kit capacity 320 is far above the draw cap, so a dozen more never hit it). People who get out are adopted as crowd walkers; people who get in are taken out of the crowd (`gone` + a 75 s cool-down on their slot).
- **The collider of a traffic car is `.traffic`, not `.vehicle`**, so `settleBody` (a driven vehicle stops dead against a `.vehicle` or `.traffic` box; it never pushes either) and the old collider-count test keep their meaning.
- **Hidden cars wait at y = -500** so a test that shows every `vehicle-*` group cannot draw the pooled ones at the origin.

## Learnings

- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · A moving obstacle sampled only at nose, middle and tail is invisible to a car crossing 3 m from either (the 10 m bus). · Sample a long vehicle every 2 m and add a point one second ahead of it when it crosses your path.
- 2026-11-28 · traffic · A lead point "one second ahead" on a car that is behind you in the same lane makes you stop for a car that is driving away. · Add the lead point only for crossing (non-parallel) vehicles.
- 2026-11-28 · crowd · A crowd slot's person is regenerated from the slot id and block, so a person who boarded the bus pops back at the stop on the next reconcile. · Hold the slot empty with a cool-down (`crowd.cool[slotId]`) and mark the member `gone`.
- 2026-11-28 · tests · A function sent with `page.evaluate(fn)` carries only its own source: constants and helpers from the spec module are not there. · Install helpers on `window` first (`tests/vehicle-life-drivers.mjs`), and keep every driver a function of at most 40 lines (lint applies to tests).
- 2026-11-28 · tests · Walkers keep re-showing after `crowd.members.forEach(m => m.show = 0)` (`show` is damped back to 1 within a few frames). · For deterministic traffic tests use a quiet hour (`setMinute(210)`), not a hidden crowd.

## Not done / risks

- **"Ride bus" as a passenger: skipped.** It needs a seat, camera and exit-at-the-next-stop flow that the bus (an ambient mover with its own lanes) does not have; it would also bring a prompt change in `interact.js`, which another workstream owns. The seated people, boarding and alighting are the visible part of the ask.
- Seated people in the bus are not visible until the looks branch makes the side windows glass (they are opaque tinted boxes today: `busWindows`). Door openings, brake / reverse / head / indicator lamps and the car doors themselves also only show once the looks branch provides `rig.doors` / `rig.lights`; until then the screenshots show the people and the scene, not the lamps and the opening door. The specs stub the rig so they test the calls.
- Cars in the kampung never drive the E-W road (above), and not at all in Madinah.
- `Rig.animateBike` passes the crank angle in radians and the wheel roll in radians; check the unit the looks branch expects.
- Boarding of a parked traffic car only starts when the child cannot see the people appear (`visibleNear`), so a hero standing right beside a parked car sees it leave only when he looks away; the tests force it (`forceBoard`).
- The exhaust shader ignores fog (the puffs are small and near). Brake lights at dusk and the open door could not be shown without the looks branch.
