## Learnings (gotchas)

- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · Two axis-aligned squares round a car's two collision circles leave a gap mid-car (r 0.8, offset 1.08: 0.56 m) a 0.66 m person nearly fits through. · One oriented box per vehicle (`Collide.orientedBox`).
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · Damping a vehicle's height toward the ground sinks the tyres into a kerb for several frames. · Snap to a plane fitted through the tyre contacts; let per-wheel suspension take up the rest.
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · A least-squares roll through two nearly collinear contacts (a bicycle steering 3 cm sideways) is ill-conditioned. · Fit roll only with 3+ contacts.
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · A rotating wheel's bounding box reads up to r(sqrt2-1) low, and a merged axle's bbox centre is mid-car. · Carry each tyre's lowest point through the wheel pivot's world matrix.
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · Mounting placed the hero at the vehicle's middle; a seat far from the middle (the lorry cab) read as "displaced" next step and threw him out. · Place him straight into the seat.
- 2026-11-28 · bus · A lane function that snaps back to the lane centre the moment a flag flips made the bus turn 90 degrees at the stop and stall. · Keep lane offsets continuous in x for every state.
- 2026-11-28 · tests · `page.evaluate(fn)` sends only `fn`'s source: helpers it calls must be on `window` or passed as source text.

## Decisions

- One oriented box per parked vehicle, kept in the shared `world.colliders` (rejected: a separate dynamic list every caller must merge). The driven vehicle is removed while driven (the hero's own settle runs while seated); the bus keeps one box updated in place.
- Speeds as ratios of the hero's run (`Sim.Vehicle.RUN` mirrors `Player.RUN`).
- Head-on bump stops a driven vehicle; a glancing touch only slows it.
- "Ground under a tyre" = highest ground in an 8 cm footprint.
- Bus is ambient only (no passenger ride): a ride needs seat / camera / exit-at-stop flows not worth the risk this round. It runs on the existing E-W road only and enters / leaves out of the hero's reach, so the HDB / hawker rework does not touch it; it needs `LAYOUT.road` (lanes at |z| 0.3 / 1.9) and `LAYOUT.busStop`.

### The rig API (the behaviour / sound workstream codes against these names)

Documented at the top of `js/figures/vehicle-rig.js`. Local frame: +z forward, +x left, y up, origin on the ground mid-wheelbase.

- `rig.doors[]`: `{ side: "L"|"R", seat, pivot, kind, open(t), t }`. Car doors `[front right, front left, rear right, rear left]`, kind `"hinge"` (65 deg about the front edge) or `"slide"` (MPV rear: steps out, runs back 0.78 m). A car / lorry door is cut out of the merged body on `open(t > 0)` and merged back at `open(0)` (+2-3 draw calls only while open). Bus: `"fold"` front (two leaves swing out), `"slide"` middle; always separate meshes. `F.Vehicles.setDoors(v, t)` (used by `bus.js`) opens every door.
- `rig.seats[]`: `{ pos: Vector3 (seated figure origin, the F.Vehicles.SEAT convention), heading, kind: "driver"|"front"|"rear"|"bus" }`. Car: driver, front, then the rear rows right / left; bus: driver + 31 passenger seats (two side-facing).
- `rig.lights`: `setBrake(on)`, `setHead(on)` (also dims the tail lamps on and lights a taxi's sign), `setIndicator("L"|"R"|"both"|null, t)` (t = a clock in seconds, blinks about 1.4 Hz; omitted = steady), `setReverse(on)`, `setRunning(on)` (DRLs), `state()` (levels: head, drl, tail, brake, indL, indR, reverse, sign). `rig.auto` (default true): `F.Vehicles.animate` sets brake (slowing, or stopped while occupied / the bus) and reverse from the speed it is given; set `rig.auto = false` to drive them yourself.
- `rig.exhaust`: `{ pos: Vector3 | null, fuel: "petrol"|"diesel"|"electric" }`; bicycles `null`. Cars petrol (2 kampung + 1 Haramain electric: `pos` null), scooter petrol, bus and lorry diesel.
- `rig.interior`: `{ wheel, seats, glass }` (dash, seats and the parked wheel are in the trim mesh; the animated wheel shows while occupied).
- `rig.wheels(spin)`, `rig.steer(angle)`; bicycles `rig.pedals(phase)`; scooters `rig.kickstand(down)` + `rig.stand`; two-wheelers `rig.helmet.wear(figureGroup)` / `.remove(figureGroup)` (fits the cast figure's head piece and hides it while worn).

### Hooks the game layer still needs (not done here, to stay out of `js/game/*`)

### Learnings

- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · A plan-view superellipse sampled at uniform z leaves the nose as one flat fan with a crease down the middle. · Sample the tip stations uniformly in width, not in length (`F.VehicleLoft.stations`).
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · Making the plan length depend on height (to lean a fascia) collapses ring points onto the centre line (a fin). · Shear the station's z by height instead; `inside()` inverts it with a few fixed-point steps.
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · Superellipse ring angles bunch the height near a = 0 (a 0.05 rad "seal" row covered 37 % of the window). · Pick ring angles by height fraction, a = asin(f^(n/2)), or width fraction, a = acos(g^(n/2)).
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · Hex vertex colours fed raw into the linear pipeline turn black tyres and trims mid grey. · Convert vehicle vertex colours sRGB to linear.
- 2026-11-28 · vehicles · A seated figure's thighs sit only about 0.27 m above its origin (`seatDrop`), so a car cushion is at 0.585 m, below the belt line. · Open the tub under the glass and line the cabin; the people inside then show.

### Decisions

- One trim material per vehicle with per-vertex classes, rather than separate lamp meshes (one call; the lamp state is two uniforms; rejected: `THREE.Light`s, per-lamp meshes, rewriting colours of shared geometry).
- Car doors split on demand (owner-bitmask variants of each merged layer, cached) rather than always separate (that would be +8-12 calls per car).
- Bus doors always separate (they open at every stop); the front door is two fold-out leaves, the middle one a sliding plug.
- The figures layer only exposes the rig; head lamps at night, helmets on mount, exhaust puffs and indicators belong to the game layer.

### Risks

- The bus is now 9 meshes (was 6): the bus-stop view measured 369 low-tier calls with it at the stop (was 371; the cars got cheaper), under the 400 line but still the tightest view.
- Bus triangles on the low tier went up (5.8k to 10.4k: seats and LED dots); cheap in calls, worth watching on very old GPUs.
- `SEAT.lorry.y` moved from 0.705 to 1.0 (the hero now sits on the cab seat); camera / exit code reading it was not changed.
- The automatic brake lamps run inside `F.Vehicles.animate`; a game layer that also sets them should set `rig.auto = false`.
- Screenshots come from software GL; clearcoat and reflections look richer on a real GPU.
