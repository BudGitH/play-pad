# Design spec — party games (Colour Rush + Snap)

**Owner:** party-games designer (PLAY-006 revamp) · **Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Scope:** `games/colour-rush/index.html`, `games/snap/index.html` only. Shared `styles/*` are **not** touched; every fix lives in the game file (game-local custom properties are fine, as Snap's `--snap-theme-*` already do).

**Method:** hands-on Playwright run of setup → countdown → in-round → taps → results → Play again, at 360×740, 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024 (touch, `isMobile`) and 1366×800 (mouse + keyboard), 2/3/4 players, dark + light, plus `reducedMotion: reduce`. `page.clock` drove the timers. **Zero console/page errors and zero horizontal overflow in every run.** All the issues below are layout, flow or clarity problems.
Screenshots: `$SHOTS` = `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-play-pad/d673e733-2e20-53e5-baf1-01b45976e8f0/scratchpad/design-party/` (named `<game>-<N>p-<viewport>-<scheme>-<step>.png`).

**Shared decisions (both games: implement once per file, keep the two consistent):**

- **Player identity = avatar + colour, never "P1/Player 1".** Auto-assign avatars 🦊 🐸 🐼 🐯 by seat. Tapping the avatar in setup cycles through a 12-animal list (no duplicates). Display name = `"🦊 Red"` everywhere: HUD, buttons, toast, results, `announce()`. This delivers the IDEAS item "Kid-friendly setup" for Priya & kid: a 7-year-old finds "the fox" without reading.
- **Colour-blind shape glyph per palette colour** (LEARNINGS PLAY-004). `SHAPES = ["●","▲","■","◆","★","✚","♥︎","✿"]` indexed like `PALETTE`, drawn on swatches, Colour Rush dots and score chips, Snap buttons and results swatches. Style it like Super Decoder's `.pegSym` (white with a dark halo).
- **Swap Pink.** `#FF375F` is almost the same as Red `#FF453A` (luminance ratio 1.03, hue 4° apart), so a Red-vs-Pink 4-player game is unreadable at a glance (`$SHOTS/colour-rush-4p-390x844-light-3round.png`, top chips). Replace it with a **light Pink `#FF9BD2`** (keep the name "Pink", because kids pick it; glyph ♥). It differs clearly from Red in lightness and from Purple in hue.
- **Ink on player colours = black.** White text on the palette measured: Yellow 1.41:1, Cyan 1.72, Green 2.02, Orange 2.06, Red 3.41, Blue 3.65, Purple/Pink 3.52. That fails AA even for large text on 4 of 8 colours (`$SHOTS/snap-2p-1366-reduced-yellow-cyan.png`). Black clears ≥ 5.7:1 on all eight. Add a game-local `--ink-on-player: #0b0b0f` and use it for all text and glyphs on player-coloured surfaces.
- **Defaults pre-selected.** Default 2 players, with colours Red / Blue / Green / Yellow pre-assigned by seat (Snap currently defaults to **4** players with nothing picked). Start is enabled on load, so a player can be in a round in about 2 taps (business outcome: playing within about 10 seconds).
- **Sticky Start bar.** Setup is 1 220–1 773px tall on phones, so Start is two screens down. Wrap `#startBtn` and `#startNote` in a `.start-bar { position: sticky; bottom: 0; padding: 12px 0 max(12px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); background: linear-gradient(transparent, var(--md-sys-color-surface) 30%) }`.
- **Taken swatches must look taken.** In light mode a swatch at 0.18 opacity reads as a pastel _choice_ (`$SHOTS/snap-3p-360x740-light-1setup.png`). Render it at 0.35 opacity with the owning player's avatar centred on it, and set `aria-label="Red — taken by 🦊"`. Swatches become **48×48** (design-system floor; 42 today).
- **Results actions:** below 420px, stack them: **Play again** (filled, full width) first, then _Change settings_ (outlined, full width). Today "Change settings" wraps to 2 lines at 360 (`$SHOTS/snap-3p-360x740-light-5results.png`). In landscape, also fix the fold problem (CR-F3).
- **Quit needs two taps.** The first tap on ✕ turns it into a pill reading "Quit?" (`--md-sys-color-error-container`) for 2 s; a second tap quits. **Esc** (desktop) opens the same confirm, and Esc again quits. In Snap the ✕ sits in the centre, where hands reach across, so one stray tap would wipe a round.
- **Pause when hidden.** On `visibilitychange` → hidden during a round, store the remaining ms, cancel rAF and timers, and show an overlay: "Paused — tap to resume". Resume re-runs a 3-2-1. Today a phone call or app switch burns the clock and the round ends unseen.
- **Haptics** (Android/Chromium only; iOS has no `navigator.vibrate`, so this is a silent no-op there). Guard with `if (soundOn && navigator.vibrate)`; the toggle is relabelled **"Sound & buzz"**. Patterns: score `12`, Snap win `[0, 30]`, wrong tap `[25, 40, 25]`, round end `60`.
- **Results delight (P2):** confetti in the winner's colour. Use 36 absolutely positioned `<i>` pieces, a 1.4 s CSS fall, removed by a JS `setTimeout`, and **skipped entirely under reduced motion** (keep the 🏆). On a tie, use mixed colours. When a player ends on 0, add the line "Great try, 🐸 Green!" (IDEAS Snap backlog).

### 1.2 Decisions

**P0**

1. **Port Snap's teardown into `show()`.** It must clear `#countdown.active`, clear a stored `countdownTimer` (new var: every `setTimeout(step…)` is assigned to it), set `running=false`, cancel rAF, call `clearDots()`, and remove confetti/pause overlays. Quit, endGame and changeBtn then only call `show()`.
2. **Measure at GO, and on every resize.** Call `measureArea()` inside the countdown `done` callback right before the first spawn. Make the resize/orientation listener run whenever `#game` is active, not only `if (running)`. On resize mid-round, also remove any live dot whose box is now outside the area.
3. **Landscape results.** Under `@media (max-height: 500px) and (orientation: landscape)`, use a two-column `.res-inner` (`max-width: 760px`). Left column: 🏆 (32px), title and sub, then the actions. Right column: the rank list (scrolls if needed). Acceptance: `#againBtn` is fully visible without scrolling at 844×390 with 4 players.

**P1**

4. Ready-note copy: **"Everyone gather round. Tap only your colour — tapping someone else's gives them the point!"**
5. At `startGame()`, paint `#sessClock` with the full duration (`1:00`) and fill the progress bar before the countdown shows.
6. **Score chips v2:** each chip is a pill with the player colour as background, `--ink-on-player` text, the content `🦊 ● 12` (avatar, shape glyph, score), a 20px/800 score, 36px height, and `gap: 8px`. With 4 players at 360 the chips wrap to 2 rows. Measure the topbar after the wrap (`measureArea` already reads `#topbar` height). The winner name on results uses the display name (`"🦊 Red wins!"`).
7. **Safe-area-aware play area:** inset `#playArea` with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (top: none, because the topbar covers it), and have `measureArea()` read `$("playArea").getBoundingClientRect()`. Then `findSpot()` works in play-area coordinates.
8. **Dots carry the shape glyph**: a centred `<span class="dsym">` at `font-size: calc(dotSize * .38)`, `--ink-on-player` with 60% alpha, `pointer-events: none`.
9. Apply the shared decisions: avatars, Pink swap, black ink, defaults, sticky Start, taken swatches, results stacking, two-tap quit + Esc, pause, haptics.

**P2** (do these: cheap and high-delight)

10. **"+1" floater:** on a tap, spawn a `+1` in the owner's colour (with a dark halo) at the dot centre. It rises 24px and fades over 600ms (reduced motion: it appears and is removed at 600ms with no movement). The owner's chip also gets a 1-frame `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px #fff` ring.
11. **Final-5-seconds tension:** at `remain ≤ 5000`, the clock turns `--md-sys-color-tertiary`, the progress fill becomes `--md-sys-color-primary`, a soft tick plays each whole second (`beep(660,.05)`), and the spawn interval drops ×0.8 ("rush hour"). At 0, a two-note "Time!" plays (`beep(988,.12)` then `beep(659,.25)`) and a 400ms "TIME!" label shows over the stage before results; paint 0:00 first (fixes CR-F9).
12. **Stage vignette:** `#game { background: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 40%, #15151d, #000 70%) }`. It is subtle, keeps contrast, and stops the stage looking empty.
13. Confetti and "Great try" on results (shared).
14. _Not now:_ a "Tiny" speed. Chill (0.75 s / 2.4 s life) tested fine for the slowest scenario. Revisit after persona feedback.

### 1.3 Implementation notes (Colour Rush)

- `show()` l.795 is the single chokepoint (LEARNINGS "UI teardown"). Do not add per-exit-path cleanup instead.
- `countdown()` l.1034 is a `setTimeout` chain with no handle. Store it (`countdownTimer = setTimeout(step, …)`) and `clearTimeout` it in `show()`. Also guard `done()` with `if (!$("game").classList.contains("active")) return;`.
- Under reduced motion, dot lifetime timers (`setTimeout(removeDot…)`) survive quit. That is harmless because `removeDot` is idempotent, but leave the guard in place.
- The floater and "TIME!" are decoration: drive their removal with `setTimeout`, never `animationend` (LEARNINGS a11y/motion row).
- `.dot.tapped` uses `animation-duration: .26s !important`, which beats the global reduced-motion collapse. Under `reduceMotion`, skip adding `tapped` and call `removeDot(d)` after 120ms instead (no burst).
- Rename `players[i].name` to the display name, and keep `aria-label`s and `srStatus` in sync.
- PALETTE changes (light Pink) must stay identical in both files; add a `// keep in sync with games/snap` comment.

### 2.2 Decisions

**P0**

1. **Re-flow the centre as a grid so nothing can overlap.** Move `#hud` and `#toast` _into_ `#center`. Make `#center` `display: grid; grid-template-rows: auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto; justify-items: center; gap: 8px`. Row 1 holds the HUD, row 2 a new `.cards` flex row with `container-type: size`, row 3 the toast (reserve `min-height: 32px` so the cards don't jump). `#center` keeps `pointer-events: none`; set `#hud { pointer-events: auto }`.
2. **Card size from the real box:** `.card-slot { width: min(calc((100cqw - var(--gap)) / 2), calc(100cqh * .75)); max-width: 220px }`. Acceptance: cards ≥ 130px wide at 360×740 (2p), ≥ 80px tall and fully visible at 844×390 (4p), and never overlapping the HUD, toast or buttons.
3. **Ink:** `.pbtn { color: var(--ink-on-player) }`. The `scored` ring stays white, but add an inner black ring (`0 0 0 4px #fff, 0 0 0 7px #000`) so it reads on Yellow/Cyan.
4. Landscape results: same two-column fix as CR P0-3.

**P1**

5. **New round cadence (fun + fairness):** round 1 keeps 3-2-1-GO at `countdownDigitMs`. **From round 2 on, there is no countdown.** After the hold, cards flip face-down for `interRoundMs`, then wait a **random 300–1100ms "shuffle" delay** (cards do a 2px wobble, which is decoration only and static under reduced motion), then flip. Normal drops to about 3.6 s per round (about 16 rounds per minute, up from 11), and the flip moment can no longer be predicted. Early taps keep giving the no-penalty `nudge`. Implement this as a new `RoundState.SHUFFLE` with `phaseDeadlineTs = now + interRoundMs + rand(300,1100)` (frantic: rand(200,600)).
6. **Gaps between buttons:** side buttons get `top/bottom: calc(var(--edge-h) + 16px)`, and the 3p top pair gets `width: calc(50% - 16px)` (8px centre gap). In landscape (`max-height: 500px`), set `--edge-h: clamp(64px, 20vh, 96px)` so the cards keep room (still well above 48px).
7. **Keyboard seats spread apart:** `DEFAULT_KEYS = ["KeyA","KeyL","KeyQ","KeyP"]`, i.e. outer home-row keys for 2p and outer top-row keys for P3/P4. Each seat has its own corner of the keyboard. Update the setup key badge. On `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, render the key on the button as a **keycap** (`min-width: 32px; height: 32px; border: 2px solid currentColor; border-radius: 8px; font: 700 18px`); on touch devices, hide it. Esc → quit confirm (shared).
8. **Toast orientation:** the "🦊 Red snapped!" text also appears _inside the winner's button_ (rotated with `.pbtn-content`, as a `.pflash` line for 650ms), so the scorer reads it facing them. The centre toast stays for "No match" / "Missed!".
9. Setup: the shared defaults (2 players, preset colours), sticky Start, taken-swatch avatars, 48px swatches. Under the Intensity chips, add the hint **"Chill suits little ones"**. Add a **"Playing solo? Try Colour Rush → / Super Decoder →"** link under the player chips (IDEAS). It is always shown and quiet (`body-small`, on-surface-variant).

**P2**

10. **SNAP! moment:** on a win, show a big "SNAP!" word (brand gradient text, `clamp(40px,14vmin,96px)`) over the cards. It is rotated to face the winner's seat (0/180/90/−90°), scales 0.6→1 in 250ms with emphasized easing, and is removed at `RESOLVE_HOLD_MS`. Under reduced motion it is static. Raise the flash opacity from 0.42 to 0.55.
11. **Reaction time:** on a win, record `performance.now() - revealTs` (not `ev.timeStamp`). The winner's button shows "0.42s" under the score for the hold. Results add one line, **"⚡ Fastest snap: 🦊 Red — 0.38 s"**.
12. **Gentle mode (kid / handicap):** a setup toggle "Gentle mode — wrong taps don't lose points". When on, a wrong tap still locks the player out for that round (fairness kept), but the score is unchanged. The cue becomes a neutral 🙈 badge, a 2-note soft `beep(440,.08)` instead of the square buzzer, and no shake. Default off. This covers the IDEAS "young-player / handicap" and "gentler cues" items with one switch.
13. Confetti, "Great try", avatars and haptics (shared).

## 3. Acceptance checks (both games; automate in the smoke spec where feasible)

1. At 360×740, 390×844, 844×390, 768×1024 and 1366×800, with 2 and 4 players, dark and light: no page/console errors, `scrollWidth === clientWidth`, and nothing overlapping. For Snap: `#hud`, `.card-slot`, `#toast` and every `.pbtn` have pairwise non-intersecting rects (≥ 4px apart). For Colour Rush: every `.dot` rect lies inside `#playArea` and below `#topbar`.
2. Fresh load → Start is enabled with no picks needed → a round is running within 2 taps. At 360×740, `#startBtn` is visible without scrolling (sticky bar).
3. Colour Rush: Start → keyboard-focus ✕ → Enter → Enter (confirm) during the 3-2-1 → the setup screen is shown with **no** overlay, and after advancing the clock 70 s it is **still on setup** with 0 `.dot` in the DOM.
4. Colour Rush: switch the viewport 390×844 → 844×390 during the countdown → after GO, every dot is fully inside the 844×390 play area.
5. At 844×390 on results with 4 players, `#againBtn` is fully within the viewport without scrolling (both games). At 360×740, both result buttons are a single line.
6. Contrast: text on every player colour ≥ 4.5:1 (computed against `--ink-on-player`). There is no Red/Pink pairing in `PALETTE`. Every dot, chip, swatch and button shows its shape glyph.
7. Snap cadence at Normal: only round 1 shows `#countdown.active`. The face-down→face-up gap between later rounds is variable (≥ 2 distinct values over 5 rounds) and lies within `interRoundMs + [300, 1100]` ms. At least 14 rounds complete in 60 s.
8. Snap keyboard: A and L score P1/P2 in 2p; Q and P score P3/P4 in 4p. Holding a key does not repeat. Esc then Esc quits to setup.
9. Snap Gentle mode on: a wrong tap leaves the score unchanged and still locks that player out until the next reveal.

11. Reduced motion: dots stay tappable for their full life. "SNAP!", "+1" and "TIME!" appear and are removed by timers. No confetti is rendered.
12. Play again from results → countdown → round, three times in a row with no stacked overlays, duplicate RAF loops (the clock must decrement at 1 s per s) or leftover confetti.
