# QA report: ACCURACY + RISK lenses (PLAY-006 revamp)

**Reviewer:** independent QA (ACCURACY + RISK) · **Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Branch:** `claude/brave-wozniak-li4rry` · **Range:** `5b41411..HEAD` (HEAD = `516bf7a`; no code changed after `f66f668`).
**Method:** read `requirements.md`, the five `design-*.md` specs, `CODE_QUALITY.md` (security + error-handling), `LEARNINGS.md`; static review of every game's state machine; then ran everything in Chromium 1243 against a QA server that serves the repo with the exact `_headers` CSP/security headers applied (the project's `tools/serve.mjs` does not apply `_headers`, so it cannot show CSP problems). Used `page.clock` for deterministic time, hostile `localStorage`, a rewritten `games.js`, forced `visibilitychange`, resizes mid-action, quit at every phase, and random-input fuzzers. No project file was edited; the QA server is stopped.

## Verified OK (probes run, no finding)

**Game rules and scoring**

- **Super Decoder `scoreGuess`:** brute-forced all 6^4 x 6^4 = 1,679,616 secret/guess pairs against an independent reference; 0 mismatches. Points `max(10, (10 - attempts + 1) * 10) + streak * 5` checked: 90, then 100/105/110 for streak 0/1/2. Best banked exactly once, synchronously at the last code's resolve (`localStorage` showed `easy: 315` while the banner was still open; quitting at that point keeps it). Racing `skipBanner()` x3 + two clicks on `#bannerCard` + the 2.4 s auto-advance advanced exactly one code. Quit mid-SUBMITTING leaves `pendingTimeouts` empty and the next session fully playable.
- **Claw Catch scoring:** forced catches gave 1, +3, +5x2 (streak 3 = x2), golden +10x2, then x3 from streak 6, all as specified (`multFor`, `catchOdds`). Golden time bonus accumulates 5 s per catch and stops at the 20 s cap (`bonusMs` 5000 to 20000, then flat). Two-prize bonus: a stacked pair banked as 2 x 1 + 3 = 5 with "DOUBLE!". Best score: quitting mid-round banks nothing, finishing a 30 s Normal round banked `30-normal` once, and a later empty-handed round neither banked nor overwrote it.
- **Claw Catch timers/pause/rotation:** hiding the tab for 20 s mid-round left `remainMs` unchanged to within one frame; hiding during the 3-2-1 restarts it with the clock still full; double hide/show events are harmless. Quit during countdown, drop, close, lift, return and release: loop stops, setup shows, restart runs exactly one loop (about 63 frames/s). Seven idle resizes plus rotation during every one of the five drop phases: no NaN prize, none outside the tray, no horizontal overflow, the carried prize still banked afterwards. A 6-round random-input fuzz (keys, Space, joystick-less, resize, hidden, quit/start, forced prizes) found no invariant break.
- **Snap fairness:** 70 taps by both seats during the random SHUFFLE wait before a match round scored nothing and locked nobody out; the first valid tap after the flip scored once, later taps in the same round were ignored. Taps during the 3-2-1, face-down gap and shuffle are no-penalty nudges. Wrong tap in a non-match round: -1 floored at 0 and the seat is locked out for the round; Gentle mode locks out without losing a point; lock-outs reset next round. Keyboard (A/L/Q/P, 4 players) scores the right seat; a key already held when the cards flip is ignored. 1-1 finish shows "It's a tie!". Pause during REVEAL abandons the round without skipping a round number, the clock stays frozen (0:55 before and after 30 s hidden), taps while paused do nothing, three rapid resume clicks start one countdown.
- **Colour Rush:** a double `pointerdown` on one dot scored once; hidden for 60 s kept the clock and cleared dots; resume = 3-2-1; Esc Esc during "TIME!" quits and the pending `showResults` timer never fires; reduced motion keeps dots tappable for their full life and removes them. "Nobody scored" and results/Play again verified.
- **Party-game fuzzers** (10 rounds each, random taps, resize, hide/show, Esc, key presses, pause clicks): after every action exactly one `.screen.active`, no dots after leaving the game, no leftover countdown/pause/SNAP! decoration, 0 page errors.
- **Neon Rush:** crash banks synchronously (coins 12 and best 141 were already in `localStorage` 100 ms after `crash()`, and quitting during the 0.95 s crash animation kept them); three back-to-back `crash()` calls banked once; results show the right numbers. Quality tiers: the auto-downgrade fired medium to low under SwiftShader (a manual `setQuality` pins the tier and clears `auto`), and cycling high/medium/low changed shadows and pixel ratio correctly with no error. Camera shows all three lanes at 320x568, 360x740, 390x844, 768x1024, 844x390 and 1366x800, and survives rotation mid-run. A 14-step random flow fuzz (start/quit/crash/retry/hoverboard) never lost or double-counted coins, never lowered the high score, and the persisted save always matched memory.

**Persistence (localStorage is a trust boundary)**

- Hub, Super Decoder, Claw Catch, Neon Rush and `theme.js` were each run with: throwing `localStorage` getter, throwing `setItem`, corrupt JSON, JSON array, 5,000-deep nested value, `"1e999"` / negative / non-numeric scores, `__proto__` / `constructor` keys, unknown ids, `playpad.theme = "purple"` / `'"dark"'`.
