### `docs/LEARNINGS.md` (append, `2026-11-21`)

- tokens: a media query cannot be combined with an attribute selector, so the light colour set exists twice in `tokens.css` (`@media (prefers-color-scheme: light) :root:not([data-theme="dark"])` and `:root[data-theme="light"]`, 31 properties, identical today). Edit both.
- theme: `scripts/theme.js` must be a classic, non-deferred `<script>` in `<head>` before the stylesheets or the page flashes the wrong theme; the smoke test asserts `data-theme` at `DOMContentLoaded`.
- file://: a directory URL shows a folder listing, so the hub's `launchHref()` appends `index.html` under `file:`; keep it when adding any link to `games/<slug>/`.
- bfcache: the hub re-reads storage on `pageshow` (`event.persisted`) or "Jump back in" and Best badges go stale after Back.
- contrast: white text on the player palette fails AA on 4 of 8 colours (Yellow 1.41:1); use black `--ink-on-player` (`#0b0b0f`). Old Pink `#FF375F` was indistinguishable from Red; palette + shapes are duplicated in Colour Rush and Snap, change both.
- hit areas: a `::after` hit-area `inset` is measured from the padding box, so a 1 px border costs 2 px (chip was 46 px, not 48). Account for the border.
- test hooks: expose live getters (Claw Catch `liveProp`), not a copied mirror, or tests race the state machine; drive rounds from the hook (`phase`, `frame`, `score`), never a fixed sleep.
- Mastermind feedback: black/white pegs are unreadable on dark; shape-code the pegs and keep the class names the tests use.
- window keydown vs focused controls: still applies (QC-01); every global key handler must bail on `closest("button")`.
- rotation: measure the play area at GO and on every resize (Colour Rush CR-F2, Claw `layoutRev`).
- cadence: a fixed 3-2-1 every round made the flip predictable and ~41% of Snap time dead; countdown only in round 1 / after pause, random shuffle delay after.
