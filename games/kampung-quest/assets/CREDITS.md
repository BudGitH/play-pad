# Kampung Quest — vendored asset credits

All assets are bundled locally so the game runs fully offline (no CDN, CSP-safe). Licence texts sit next to the files.

## Textures

Photographic PBR materials from **ambientCG** (ambientcg.com), **CC0 1.0** (`textures/LICENSE.txt`). Downloaded as the 1K-JPG sets and resized to JPEG (1024 px for large surfaces, 512 px for minor ones). Colour maps are sRGB; `_n` are OpenGL normal maps, `_r` roughness, `_a` alpha (opacity) masks. Total ≈ 2.6 MB.

## Prayer times

The 26 November 2026 Singapore times in `js/data/prayer-times.js` come from the **MUIS (Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura) "Muslim Prayer Timetable 2026"**, published on data.gov.sg (dataset `d_d441e7242e78efc566024dd5b0d9829c`, https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_d441e7242e78efc566024dd5b0d9829c/view; last updated 2026-04-02; retrieved 2026-11-26). The same agency's consolidated dataset (`d_a6a206cba471fe04b62dd886ef5eaf22`) gives identical values. data.gov.sg content is published under the Singapore Open Data Licence (https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence). The times are fixed in the game: no network, no geolocation.
